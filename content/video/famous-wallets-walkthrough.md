# Famous Wallets Walkthrough — shot list + VO script

Target: ~40s product walkthrough for the dashboard spotlight popup
(`/public/videos/famous-wallets.mp4`), reusable as an IG reel and X post.
Eduardo captures the shots below (screen recording at 1080p+, or stills
with slow cursor drift added in the edit); VO is TTS (ash), captions on.
Keep each shot a beat longer than feels right — the cut trims, never
stretches.

Music: low-key pulse, no drop. Cursor: slow, deliberate, one action per
shot. Never move and click in the same breath.

---

**SHOT 1 — The hook (4s)**
Capture: `/figures` — the "Famous wallets" rail at the top. Slow pan or
cursor drift across the avatars (Vitalik, MrBeast, Binance, SpaceX…).
No clicks.
VO: "Every crypto wallet is public. Even theirs."

**SHOT 2 — Pick a person (4s)**
Capture: cursor drifts to **Vitalik Buterin**, hovers half a second,
clicks. Page transition to /figure/vitalik-buterin.
VO: "This is Vitalik's actual wallet. Verified on-chain."

**SHOT 3 — The money shot (7s)**
Capture: /figure/vitalik-buterin hero — hold on the **Portfolio value
stat card + the return chips (24H / 7D / 30D / 1Y)**, then slow scroll
to bring the **Portfolio value · 1y chart** fully into frame. Let the
chart breathe 2–3s. No clicks.
VO: "Four hundred twenty-seven million dollars. Up or down, every single
day, charted for the last year."

**SHOT 4 — What did they DO (6s)**
Capture: scroll to the recent-transactions feed on the same page.
Cursor points (no click) at one large row — ideally a labeled
counterparty ("Coinbase", "Uniswap").
VO: "See exactly what they bought, when they moved it, and who was on
the other side."

**SHOT 5 — Profit or loss (6s)**
Capture: the embedded **backtest panel** on the figure page, already
run: the equity curve + return figure visible. Cursor circles the
percentage once, slowly.
VO: "Sonar even replays their trades — so you know if they actually
made money."

**SHOT 6 — Breadth flex (5s)**
Capture: back on `/figures`, slow scroll down the directory — the sheer
number of names. (Alt: the entity page "Top Counterparties" card for
Binance.)
VO: "Athletes. Billionaires. Governments. Two hundred fifty of the most
famous wallets on earth."

**SHOT 7 — CTA (4s)**
Capture: clean still of the dashboard with the Sonar logo visible, or
the /figures hero.
VO: "Free at sonartracker dot io. Watch the whales."
On-screen text: **sonartracker.io/figures**
