**Type**: meme cinemagraph (~$0.85 budget: 1 Seedance i2v clip over existing
meme art, no new stills). **Asset**: `content/ig/assets/meme-emptypockets.jpg`
(existing — an orca-mascot character turning out its empty trouser pockets
at a lantern-lit fantasy bazaar, two other whale-shoppers in the background,
hot-air balloons overhead — soft-shaded illustration style, on-brand, not
the frog).

**Seedance i2v motion prompt** (image-to-video, 5s, 1080p):
> Animate this illustrated scene of a whale character checking its empty
> trouser pockets at a bustling fantasy bazaar. Subtle, slow, continuous
> looping motion only: the whale's shoulders sink slightly and its head
> tilts down a touch further in mild despondence; the loose pocket fabric
> sways gently in a light breeze; the small dropped coin on the cobblestones
> gives one last gentle spin and settles; the distant hot-air balloons drift
> slowly across the sky; the market lanterns overhead sway gently; soft
> ambient dust motes catch the golden light. Keep the two background whale
> shoppers mostly static, only slight idle breathing motion. Cinematic,
> slow, continuous movement — no camera cuts, no fast motion, no new
> objects or characters entering frame — subtle enough to loop seamlessly.

**Overlay beats** (Anton, transparent PNG, headless-Chrome-rendered, `-loop 1
-t <dur>` per still per runbook):
1. `0.0–2.5s`: "EVERYONE'S POCKETS WENT EMPTY THIS WEEK."
2. `2.5–5.0s`: "EXCEPT THE WHALES'."
3. End card (`5.0–8.5s`, shared end-card template): Sonar logo + "WHALES
   BOUGHT $487M OF BTC IN 24H" + "Comment \"WHALE\" · sonartracker.io"

**Silent by design** — the meme-cinemagraph format reads fine on mute with
overlay text carrying the joke, matching runbook guidance that narration is
optional for this reel type and should only be added where it clearly fits.
No VO script is required today; if Eduardo wants one anyway, a one-line
Orca aside over the end card ("...I'm just saying — comment WHALE for the
receipts.") would work without rewriting the clip.

Target total length ~8s (5s clip + ~3-3.5s end-card hold) — a touch under
the 7-15s "ideal" range in the runbook's growth playbook, but appropriate
for a punchy single-beat gag; the local session can extend the end-card
hold to 5-6s (total ~10-11s) if a longer loop performs better, without
needing new footage.
