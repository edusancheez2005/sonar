## Stories (WebSearch-verified only — no Binance/Sonar API access)

**1. The Binance hack (lead) — the biggest crypto exchange hack ever recorded.** Roughly
**$6.36B** moved out of Binance's wallets across **10 blockchains**, most of it in about
**20 minutes** starting ~20:05 UTC on 29 Oct 2026 (TRM Labs). Binance traced the attack vector
to a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop / a breach of its hot-wallet signing
infrastructure. Blockchain-intelligence firms TRM Labs and Chainalysis both assessed, with
stated high confidence, that the pattern matches **TraderTraitor**, a North Korea-linked
hacking unit generally described as a Lazarus Group subunit; independent researcher ZachXBT
called it "likely Lazarus." CoinDesk's own reporting is more hedged — attribution is not yet
a confirmed/official finding, only an analyst assessment — so every slide uses "suspected" /
"the pattern matches," never a flat claim. Binance said it will cover all losses and
temporarily suspended deposits/withdrawals while investigating; one report says roughly half
the assets have since been recovered, though recovering the BTC/ETH portion is harder than
other assets. Bitcoin fell **below $77,000** within hours of the news spreading (multiple
independent outlets, including a dedicated "bitcoin price falls below 77000" piece);
reporting also put the broader crypto market cap falling toward **~$2.67T**, though this
specific figure carries lower confidence than the hack total (see rejected/lower-confidence
numbers below).

**2. (Not used as a standalone post — logged for tomorrow) Binance hack secondary effects**:
