Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal data when you visit www.roscap.co.uk, contact us, or engage with our services.

Our business

Roscap intermediates and advises on unregulated real estate financing transactions, globally and predominantly in the United Kingdom, including bridging finance, development finance, renovation finance, buy-to-let mortgages, and commercial mortgages.

Key principles

  • We only collect personal data that we need for defined purposes.
  • We use personal data lawfully, fairly, and transparently in line with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
  • We apply appropriate security measures and limit access to authorised personnel and service providers.
  • We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out below and to meet legal, accounting, or compliance obligations.

Personal data we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact data – name, company name, job title, email address, telephone number, postal address.
  • Business and transaction data – information you provide about assets, projects, funding requirements, counterparties, and related documentation.
  • Due diligence data – where required for compliance: identification documents, proof of address, beneficial ownership information, source of funds/wealth information, and screening results (e.g. sanctions/PEP checks).
  • Partner data – if you join our Partner Program: your contact details, the clients you introduce to us, and the details we need to pay your commission.
  • Technical and usage data – IP address, device and browser information, pages visited, and interactions with the website (subject to your cookie preferences).
  • Marketing preferences – whether you opt in or opt out of communications.

How we collect data

  • Directly from you (forms on our website, email, telephone, messaging platforms, meetings, or introductions).
  • From counterparties and professional advisers involved in a transaction (e.g. lenders, brokers, valuers, solicitors).
  • From public sources and databases for due diligence (e.g. Companies House) and screening providers.

Purposes and lawful bases

We process personal data for the purposes below, using one or more lawful bases under UK GDPR:

  • To respond to enquiries and provide our services (steps prior to entering a contract / performance of a contract).
  • To manage relationships with clients, lenders, and investors (legitimate interests).
  • To run our Partner Program, including recording introductions and paying commissions (performance of a contract and legitimate interests).
  • To perform compliance checks, including AML/KYC and sanctions screening where applicable (legal obligation and/or legitimate interests).
  • To operate, secure, and improve our website (legitimate interests; and consent where cookies are not strictly necessary).
  • To send marketing communications (consent where required; otherwise legitimate interests where lawful).
  • To meet legal, regulatory, and record-keeping obligations (legal obligation).
  • To establish, exercise, or defend legal claims (legitimate interests / legal claims).

Where we rely on legitimate interests, those interests are running and developing our business, managing our relationships and records, and keeping our business and website secure. You can object to this processing at any time (see “Your rights”).

Website analytics and advertising measurement

With your consent, given through the cookie banner on our website, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how our website is used and Google Ads conversion measurement to learn whether enquiries come from our online adverts. These services are provided by Google and place the cookies listed in our Cookie Policy. The information processed includes pages visited, approximate location derived from your IP address (the IP address itself is not stored by Google Analytics 4), device and browser details, how you reached the site and whether you sent an enquiry. We do not use these services to show you personalised adverts and we have not enabled Google Signals.

Lawful basis: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time using the Cookie preferences link at the foot of every page; this does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it. If you do not consent, the website works in the same way and only strictly necessary cookies are used.

Data retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. Transaction and due diligence records may be retained for longer periods where required by legal, accounting, or compliance obligations, or to manage disputes. We periodically review and securely delete or anonymise data that is no longer required.

As a guide:

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a mandate: 2 years from our last contact.
  • Client, lender and investor records for a mandate, including due diligence records: 5 years after the transaction or the business relationship ends.
  • Accounting and tax records: 6 years from the end of the financial year they relate to.
  • Contracts, including Partner Program agreements and commission records: 6 years after the agreement ends.
  • Marketing contacts: until you unsubscribe; we keep a short record so that we do not contact you again.
  • Website analytics (only with your consent): 14 months.
  • Your cookie choice: 6 months, after which we ask again.
