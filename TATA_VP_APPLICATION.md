### What is your business idea, what is the problem it will solve? — *93 words / 100 max*

Crypto traders lose money every day because the information they need is too expensive, scattered or unreliable. I lived this as a trader: Whale Alert for transactions, LunarCrush for sentiment, CoinGecko for prices, Twitter for news, and I'd still find out hours late when a whale moved $10 million to an exchange. Sonar fixes that. We track whale transactions above $500K in real time across major blockchains, fuse six data sources into one confidence-scored sentiment rating, and let traders ask our AI advisor, Orca, questions in plain English. One dashboard, £7.99 a month.

### Why is it different to what has come before? — *100 words / 100 max*

Our competitors each solve a piece of the puzzle. Nansen does on-chain analytics but charges $150 a month with no AI. Arkham is built for researchers, not everyday traders. LunarCrush does social sentiment but ignores whale activity. Dune needs SQL. Whale Alert gives raw data with zero context. Sonar is the only platform that combines whale tracking, sentiment, news and an AI advisor for under £8 a month. Longer term, our moat is the data itself: we're training models on our own signal history and whale movement data. An AI quant analyst that gets smarter over time, priced for retail.

### Who are the customers and what have you done to show they need your solution? — *97 words / 100 max*

Semi-experienced retail traders: one to three years in crypto, portfolios between £1K and £100K. Through King's Idea Factory I interviewed over 20 active traders, and the pattern was consistent: frustrated by fragmented tools, priced out of professional ones ($150 a month is 3% of a £5K portfolio, every month, just for analytics). Validation so far: 850+ registered users on sonartracker.io, 150 of them in two weeks with zero marketing spend; five paying subscribers through Stripe; over 600 traders on our weekly whale-report email; £2,200 won across Idea Factory and Open Pitch Night. We won the Idea Factory finals.

### Where is the market, how big is it and are there any competitors? — *95 words / 100 max*

There are over 420 million crypto holders globally and an estimated 50 to 80 million active traders; our initial focus is the 20 to 30 million English-speaking ones. The crypto analytics market was worth $2.2 billion in 2024 and is projected to reach $8.6 billion by 2030 (25% CAGR). Investors have already validated the space: Nansen raised $75 million, Dune reached a $1 billion valuation, Arkham launched its own token. But all of them chase institutions and researchers. The affordable retail segment, the majority of actual traders, is wide open, and that's where Sonar sits.

### How will you make money and be sustainable? — *247 words / 250 max*

Sonar is freemium SaaS. The free tier is deliberately generous because it maximises the top of the funnel. Premium is £7.99 a month and unlocks full whale tracking, signals and unlimited Orca questions. Free users get one Orca question a day, which is our best conversion hook, because it's the feature traders get most excited about. Stripe is already processing real payments.

The unit economics work at small scale. Operating costs are around £500 a month across data APIs, Vercel, Supabase and AI inference, so we break even at roughly 63 subscribers. Costs fall from there as we replace third-party data APIs with our own pipelines; six already run in production.

Pricing will evolve as we learn willingness to pay at different portfolio sizes. For a trader with a £5K portfolio, £7.99 is 0.16% of holdings per month. Nansen's $150 would be 3%, which makes no sense for them. That gap is our wedge. As we grow: a Pro tier at £19.99 to £29.99 for larger-portfolio traders who want deeper analytics and API access, then a public API tier, and eventually data licensing, because our accumulated whale-movement and signal history becomes more valuable the longer we run.

Acquisition stays cheap. Organic growth through crypto communities on X, Reddit and Discord added 150 users in two weeks with zero spend. On top of that: a weekly whale-intelligence email keeping free users warm, SEO through our blog and glossary pages, and a short-form video series that funnels viewers to Sonar.

### When is this going to happen — timeline, plan, risks, team, resources? — *248 words / 250 max*

This is already happening. Sonar has been live at sonartracker.io since early 2026: 850+ users, five paying subscribers, and six automated data pipelines in production tracking 100+ tokens across Ethereum, Solana and Base.

Next six months: ship the backtesting engine publicly so traders can validate our signals against history before risking money. Simplify onboarding, because users who don't understand the dashboard in the first 30 seconds bounce. Launch the short-form video series and the Sonar Discord. Push conversion towards the 2 to 5% freemium benchmark. By twelve months: 5,000 active users, 250+ paying subscribers, recurring revenue covering costs with margin, a public API, and a clear path to pre-seed investment.

Team: I'm a Computer Science with Management student at King's and I built the platform from scratch, after an AI internship in the UAE and time at Microsoft. My co-founder Saif, CS and Maths at King's, leads our Bitcoin and Solana data engineering. Between us we cover full-stack, AI and blockchain development without needing to hire.

The risks, honestly. Our riskiest assumption is that fusing data sources measurably improves trading outcomes; the backtesting engine exists to prove or disprove that with hard numbers. User acquisition is my bottleneck: I'm a strong builder who needs to become a stronger marketer, and that's where prize money and mentoring would go. Nansen could launch a retail tier, but a £7.99 product cannibalises their enterprise pricing; for us it's the whole strategy. We provide data, not financial advice, inside FCA and ASA guidance.
