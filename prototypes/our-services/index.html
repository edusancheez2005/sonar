Our Services

Roscap provides structured real estate capital solutions across the full capital stack: from senior debt to co-investment equity.

We advise developers, investors, and institutions on structuring and securing capital that aligns with both the project and the market.

Our role is to simplify complexity, deliver clarity and ensure each transaction is executed with precision.

Our expertise

Capital Raising Across the Real Estate Spectrum

We arrange debt and equity across planning, development and investment strategies.

Our clients include property developers, investors, and family offices seeking institutional-quality structuring and execution.

Each transaction is tailored to the asset, the capital structure, and the long-term objectives of our clients and their funding partners.

Debt Funding

Real Estate Debt Advisory

We advise on and arrange senior, stretch-senior, and mezzanine debt across a range of real estate strategies.

Our understanding of the lending market allows us to identify and negotiate suitable structures, balancing leverage, cost, and flexibility.

Roscap’s experience covers:

Development Finance

Funding for ground-up, conversion or renovation projects.

Bridging Finance

Short-term funding for acquisitions, refinancing, or equity release.

Commercial and
BTL Mortgages

Income-based term lending for stabilised assets.

Mezzanine Finance

Subordinated capital to enhance leverage or bridge equity requirements.

Debt can be structured to suit each stage of a project, from site acquisition to stabilisation.

Equity  Funding

Equity and Co-Investment

We raise and structure equity for developers, sponsors, and family offices seeking joint ventures, preferred equity, or direct co-investment.

Our investor base includes private and institutional partners seeking exposure to UK, Europe, and North America real estate through aligned, professionally structured opportunities.

Roscap provides access to:

Preferred Equity

Fixed-return positions behind senior debt with potential profit participation.

Joint Venture Equity

Shared-risk structures with aligned upside.

Corporate Capital Raising

For operating businesses with real estate holdings.

We focus on transparency and alignment between sponsor and investor, ensuring objectives are consistent from entry to exit.
