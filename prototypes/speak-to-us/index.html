FAQs

What does Roscap do as a real estate capital advisory firm?

Roscap sources and structures property finance, including development finance, bridging loans, commercial mortgages, mezzanine finance, and preferred equity. We work with UK and international lenders to secure competitive terms for residential, commercial, and mixed-use real estate projects.

How quickly can Roscap arrange development finance or a bridging loan?

For urgent acquisitions, Roscap can secure bridging loans in as little as 5–10 working days, subject to valuation and legal readiness. Development finance and commercial mortgages typically complete within 6–8 weeks, depending on lender due diligence and asset complexity.

What types of projects and assets does Roscap fund?

We arrange funding for residential developments, BTR schemes, commercial buildings, mixed-use projects, hotel and leisure assets, industrial/logistics units, and specialist sectors such as supported living and student accommodation. We also fund international projects through our global capital network.

What information do I need to apply for property finance through Roscap?

To underwrite a deal, we typically require a summary of the asset, development appraisals or financial models, borrower background, planning status, valuations, rent roll (for income assets), and exit strategy. Clear information helps us source faster, sharper terms from our lending partners.

Does Roscap work with both UK and international lenders?

Yes. Roscap works with UK challenger banks, private credit funds, family offices, specialist lenders, and institutional capital, alongside European and US investors. This global network enables us to structure bespoke, competitive finance for both UK and international real estate projects
