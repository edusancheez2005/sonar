Roscap Limited (company number 13536901) ('Roscap', 'we', 'us' or 'our').

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies are, how we use them on www.roscap.co.uk, and the choices you have. Non-essential cookies are used only where you have provided consent via our cookie banner.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies (such as pixels and local storage) may also be used to store or access information on your device.

Your choices

When you first visit, a banner asks whether we may use optional cookies. Nothing optional is set until you choose. You can accept all, reject all non-essential cookies, or choose by category under Manage preferences. Your choice is stored in a cookie called roscap_consent for 6 months, after which we ask again. We also ask again if we change the cookies we use or the purposes we use them for.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the Cookie preferences link at the foot of every page. Withdrawing consent stops the optional cookies being used from that point and removes the Google cookies we control from your browser; it does not affect data collected while consent was in place.

Cookie categories we use

  • Strictly necessary - required for the website to function (e.g., security, load balancing, consent preferences). These do not require consent.
  • Analytics - help us understand how the website is used so we can improve it. Used only if you consent. We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google, for example to see which pages are read and how people arrive at the site. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. The data is shared with Google, which may process it in the United States (see the International transfers section of our Privacy Policy). Reports are kept in our Google Analytics account for 14 months. Google Signals is switched off, so this data is not combined with Google account information.
  • Marketing measurement - if you arrive from one of our Google Ads adverts and then send an enquiry, Google Ads conversion measurement tells us that the enquiry came from that advert. We will only use these if you consent. We use them only to measure whether our advertising works, not for remarketing or to show you personalised adverts.
