Debt Funding

Real Estate Debt Advisory

We advise on and arrange senior, stretch-senior, and mezzanine debt across a range of real estate strategies.

Our understanding of the lending market allows us to identify and negotiate suitable structures, balancing leverage, cost, and flexibility.

Roscap’s experience covers:

Development Finance

Funding for ground-up, conversion or renovation projects.

Bridging Finance

Short-term funding for acquisitions, refinancing, or equity release.

Commercial and
BTL Mortgages

Income-based term lending for stabilised assets.

Mezzanine Finance

Subordinated capital to enhance leverage or bridge equity requirements.

Our Process

A Structured Approach to Every Transaction

Each mandate follows a clear and consistent process to ensure transparency and timely completion.

  1. 1

    Initial review

    Pre-empt capital partner questions and analyse the transaction.

  2. 2

    Engagement

    Client signs our engagement letter, authorising Roscap to represent them.

  3. 3

    Capital markets

    Create presentation materials, engage funders and present the deal.

  4. 4

    Review offers

    Organise the terms in a coherent format and present to the client, advising on the pros and cons.

  5. 5

    Negotiation

    Re-engage shortlisted funders to fine tune the offers.

  6. 6

    Deal Closing

    Finalise offer and intimately manage the transaction through application, due diligence and completion.
