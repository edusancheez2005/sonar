`shared/` holds the brand tokens and base styles rebuilt from the live stylesheet (base.css), the shared nav and footer markup, fonts (Boska, Switzer), images taken from the owner's concept files, and the small shared script (nav, reveals, mock forms). The nav copies the live one, measured on roscap.co.uk: a rounded bar inset from the edges of the screen, light glass at the top of the page and solid teal once the page has scrolled 80% of the screen height (always solid on the legal pages and the 404); on a computer, scrolling down fades the page links out and leaves the logo and Speak to Us, scrolling up brings them back. That is the live nav as it is, so there is nothing to change in Webflow for it. Forms in the prototypes are switched off; nothing is sent anywhere.

Rules every prototype follows: no copy that states or implies FCA authorisation; British English, digits for numbers, no em dashes, no exclamation marks; lighter than the live page it replaces; prefers-reduced-motion respected; no scroll-jacking; CSS scroll-driven animation first, GSAP only where needed, no Lenis.

QA and verification reports are in `../audit/qa/` (the `-v1-superseded` files cover the first Partner Program and Speak to us versions, replaced after the owner's review). Items marked CONFIRM in a prototype or its handoff need Clemente's sign-off before publishing. Wording on the live site is kept exactly as Roscap wrote it (owner's decision, 31 October).
